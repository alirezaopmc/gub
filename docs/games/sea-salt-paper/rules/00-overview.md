# Sea Salt & Paper: Overview

In **Sea Salt & Paper** (designed by Bruno Cathala & Théo Rivière), players collect origami sea creatures, play duo pairs for immediate tactical effects, and time the end of the round with precision.

## Turn Flow

On your turn, you must take cards through one of two choices:
1. **Draw from deck:** Draw 2 cards from the face-down draw pile, choose 1 to keep in your hand, and discard the other face-up to either of the two discard piles.
2. **Take from discard:** Pick the top card from either of the two open discard piles into your hand.

After drawing, you may optionally place down any number of **Duo pairs** to activate their powers.

## Ending the Round (The 7-Point Mark)

When you have **7 or more points** in hand and played duos, you may end the round at the end of your turn by making a declaration:

1. **STOP:** The round ends immediately. All players reveal hands and score all of their points.
2. **LAST CHANCE:** You wager that you will have strictly more points than every single opponent. Each opponent takes one final turn.
   - If you win the bet: You score all your points plus your Color Bonus. Opponents score **only** their Color Bonus.
   - If you lose the bet: You score **only** your Color Bonus. Opponents score all of their points.

## Winning the Game

The game is played over multiple rounds until a player reaches the target score:
- **2 players:** 40 points
- **3 players:** 35 points
- **4 players:** 30 points

**Instant Win:** If any player collects **all 4 Mermaids**, that player immediately wins the entire game on the spot, regardless of current scores!
