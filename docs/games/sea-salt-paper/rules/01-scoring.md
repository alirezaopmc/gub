# Scoring Guide

## 1. Duo Pairs (1 Point each + Power)

Placing a duo pair on the table immediately grants 1 point and its ability:
- **2 Crabs:** Search either discard pile and pick any 1 card into hand.
- **2 Boats:** Take another turn immediately.
- **2 Fish:** Draw the top card from the deck into hand.
- **Swimmer + Shark:** Steal 1 random card from an opponent's hand.

## 2. Set Collections (Scored in hand)

- **Shells (6 cards in deck):**
  - 1 Shell: 0 points
  - 2 Shells: 2 points
  - 3 Shells: 4 points
  - 4 Shells: 6 points
  - 5 Shells: 9 points
  - 6 Shells: 12 points

- **Octopuses (5 cards in deck):**
  - 1 Octopus: 0 points
  - 2 Octopuses: 1 point
  - 3 Octopuses: 3 points
  - 4 Octopuses: 6 points
  - 5 Octopuses: 9 points

- **Penguins (3 cards in deck):**
  - 1 Penguin: 1 point
  - 2 Penguins: 3 points
  - 3 Penguins: 5 points

- **Sailors (2 cards in deck):**
  - 1 Sailor: 0 points
  - 2 Sailors: 5 points

## 3. Multipliers

- **Lighthouse:** +1 point per Boat card you have (including those played in pairs).
- **Shoal of Fish:** +1 point per Fish card you have (including those played in pairs).
- **Penguin Colony:** +2 points per Penguin card you have.
- **Captain:** +3 points per Sailor card you have.

## 4. Mermaids

- **1st Mermaid:** 1 point per card of your most numerous color.
- **2nd Mermaid:** 1 point per card of your 2nd most numerous color.
- **3rd Mermaid:** 1 point per card of your 3rd most numerous color.
- **4 Mermaids:** Instant Game Win!
