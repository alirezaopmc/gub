# Scout: Overview

In **Scout**, players take on the role of circus directors scouting performers to build the most spectacular show in town.

## The Cardinal Rule: Fixed Hand Order

When cards are dealt at the start of a round:
1. You **may not reorder** any cards in your hand.
2. Before the round begins, you may inspect your hand and choose to **flip the entire hand upside down** once. Whichever orientation you choose stays fixed for the remainder of the round.
3. Every card has two numbers (top and bottom). Only the number in the top position is active while in hand.

## End of Round

A round ends immediately when either:
1. **Empty Hand:** Any player plays the last card from their hand via a **Show** or **Scout & Show**.
2. **Table Cleared:** A player plays a combination, and every single opponent scouts (passes) without anyone being able to beat it. When the turn returns to the owner of that combination, the round ends.

## Number of Rounds

A full match lasts as many rounds as there are players (e.g. 3 rounds for 3 players, 4 rounds for 4 players). The player with the highest cumulative score at the end wins!
