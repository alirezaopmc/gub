# Actions

On your turn, you must take exactly one of three actions:

## 1. Show

Play a valid combination of cards from your hand to the table.
- Selected cards must be **contiguous** (adjacent) in your hand.
- Valid combinations:
  - **Single:** 1 card.
  - **Set:** 2 or more cards with identical numbers (e.g., 5-5-5).
  - **Run:** 2 or more cards in consecutive ascending or descending order (e.g., 2-3-4 or 7-6-5).
- Your combination must be strictly stronger than the active combination currently on the table:
  - More cards beat fewer cards.
  - Equal card counts: Sets beat Runs.
  - Equal combination types: Higher value wins.
- **Reward:** Take all cards from the beaten table combination and place them face-down in your score pile (+1 point per card).

## 2. Scout

If you cannot or choose not to beat the table combination:
- Take either the **leftmost** or **rightmost** card from the active combination on the table.
- Choose whether to keep its top or bottom number active.
- Insert the card into **any position** in your hand (this is how you curate sets and runs!).
- **Reward:** The player who played the table combination receives **+1 Scout Chip** (+1 point).

## 3. Scout & Show (Once per round)

Once per round, you may spend your Scout & Show token to:
1. Scout a card into your hand according to the normal Scout rules.
2. Immediately perform a **Show** in the very same turn.
