# Scoring

At the end of each round, every player tallies their round score using this simple formula:

$$\text{Score} = \text{Cards Collected} + \text{Scout Chips} - \text{Cards Left in Hand}$$

## Breakdown:

| Category | Points | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Cards in Score Pile** | **+1 point** each | Cards collected by beating previous table combinations. |
| **Scout Chips** | **+1 point** each | Chips earned when opponents scouted cards from your combinations. |
| **Cards Left in Hand** | **-1 point** each | Penalty for every card remaining in your hand at round end. |

## The Table-Cleared Exemption

If a round ends because **every opponent scouted** and the turn returned to you without your combination being beaten:
- You receive all points for cards collected and chips.
- You are **exempt from hand penalties**: you do **not** lose points for any cards remaining in your hand!
- All other players must still deduct 1 point per card left in their hand.
