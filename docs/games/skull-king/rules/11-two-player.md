# Two-player Graybeard

Skull King is officially designed for **2–8 players** (rulebook p.21). With 2 players, the game introduces a ghost player named **Graybeard** to simulate a 3-player trick-taking dynamic.

## Setup

- **Loot cards are removed** from the deck in 2-player games (alliances require at least 3 players).
- Each round, deal the round's hand size to both human players and deal an identical face-down stack to **Graybeard**.
- Graybeard's hand remains face-down as a draw pile.

## Bidding

- Both human players bid as normal.
- **Graybeard does not bid** and cannot earn points.

## Trick play

1. **Turn order:**
   - On the first trick of each round, the human leader leads first.
   - **Graybeard always plays second**, flipping the top card of his face-down hand into the trick.
   - The other human player plays third (last).
2. **Graybeard's card:**
   - Graybeard plays blind from the top of his stack and does **not** have to follow suit.
   - If Graybeard plays the **Tigress**, it is **always played as an Escape** (never as a Pirate).
   - If Graybeard plays a pirate, its special ability is not activated.
3. **If Graybeard wins the trick:**
   - Neither human player receives the trick.
   - Graybeard leads the next trick by flipping his next top card. The human player to his left follows, and the second human player plays last.

## Scoring

- Human players score according to standard rules (Classic or Rascal's Scoring).
- Any 14 bonus or capture bonus in a trick won by Graybeard is discarded and awarded to no one.
